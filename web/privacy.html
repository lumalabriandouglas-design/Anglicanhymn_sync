Privacy Policy

Anglican Hymn Sync. Last updated 7 October 2026.

No accounts

The app has no sign-up, login or user accounts.

No personal data collected

We do not collect, store, sell or share personal information. Your favourites, setlists and settings are saved only on your own device, and uninstalling the app or clearing site data removes them.

Audio and content

Hymn recordings and the audio catalogue stream from a public content delivery network (Cloudflare R2). As with any web request, that network may see your IP address and basic device information to deliver the file. We do not use it to identify or track you.

No ads or tracking

The app has no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and knowingly collects no data from anyone, including children.
